Her Story

From the cold streets
to center stage.

Born in St. Petersburg with ice-blue eyes and a voice that cuts through silence, LouM knew music was her destiny from the moment she sang her first note. Raised on classical training and street pop, she fused two worlds into one unmistakable sound.

Her debut single broke streaming records across Europe in under 72 hours. Since then, she hasn't stopped. Three albums, four world tours, and a presence that fills every room she walks into.

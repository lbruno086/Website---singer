Her Story

From Paris
to Buenos Aires.

Born in Paris, France, LouM is a 34-year-old pop rock artist, songwriter, and performer now based in Buenos Aires. Fluent in music across multiple languages, she brings a rare cosmopolitan depth to every performance — from intimate piano-driven shows to full live productions.

A true composer at heart, she wrote and recorded her debut original single "Show Me" in English — a bold first statement that has already taken her to stages across Buenos Aires. On stage she performs alongside her pianist, creating an intimate yet powerful sound that is entirely her own.
